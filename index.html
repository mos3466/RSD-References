Research supporting AI-assisted human action recognition, computer vision, and performance evaluation for intelligent public transportation monitoring.

About RSD

RSD is a prototype intelligent monitoring system designed to identify potential public transportation violations, including eating, drinking, and sitting on the floor.

The system combines camera-based detection, RFID trip identification, and human verification to support monitoring decisions.

The following publications provide scientific background relevant to the project's research direction. They do not necessarily represent models implemented in the current prototype.

01 | Human Action Recognition

NTU RGB+D 120: A Large-Scale Benchmark for 3D Human Activity Understanding

J. Liu et al., IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, 2020.

A large-scale benchmark for recognizing human activities, including eating, drinking, and sitting-related actions. It provides research background for understanding human behavior from visual and motion information.

02 | Pose Estimation

Realtime Multi-Person 2D Pose Estimation Using Part Affinity Fields

Z. Cao, T. Simon, S.-E. Wei, and Y. Sheikh, IEEE CVPR, 2017.

This research presents an approach for estimating body joint positions for multiple people in images. Pose information can support the analysis of human movement and posture.

03 | Performance Evaluation

A Systematic Analysis of Performance Measures for Classification Tasks

M. Sokolova and G. Lapalme, Information Processing & Management, 2009.

This paper examines evaluation measures for classification systems. It supports the selection of metrics such as Precision, Recall, and F1-score for future controlled evaluation of RSD.
